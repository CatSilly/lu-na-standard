# LNS Scoring System

| Score | Rating |
|------:|--------|
| < 50 | Terrible |
| 50 – 69 | Fair |
| 70 – 79 | Good |
| 80 – 89 | Great |
| 90 – 99 | Outstanding |
| 100 | Beyond Perfection |

> [!NOTE]
> A score below 50 doesn't necessarily mean the project/product is terrible; it's just a personal assessment.
