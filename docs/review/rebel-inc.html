Review

This is one of my favorite games by Ndemic Creations. When I finally completed a playthrough on Brutal difficulty—the highest difficulty available in the free version—it genuinely felt like I'd just won a real battle. The sense of accomplishment was incredible. Earlier on, though, I struggled because I hadn't taken the time to learn the game's tactics and strategies, so I kept losing on the later maps. Even so, Rebel Inc. remains one of my favorite games, right after Plague Inc., because the two games have completely different art styles while both offering unique and engaging gameplay.
