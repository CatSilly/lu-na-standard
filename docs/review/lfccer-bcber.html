Review

This is easily one of my favorite rhythm games. Even though the gameplay pretty much boils down to "dodge obstacles to the beat," it has this inexplicable charm that I cant quite put into words. Another thing that caught me off guard was the graphics... its literally just a bunch of rainbow-colored geometric shapes, like the developer was too lazy to do any actual decorating. That said, there are definitely a few areas that need some work: the color schemes for the built-in levels feel a bit too similar and need more variety, the visual design is inconsistent (for instance, the play button in the main menu looks super detailed while the level dashboard looks pretty rough), and there are a few minor bugs that should be fixed soon. Overall, though, its well worth playing if youre willing to overlook a few small flaws and want to experience the sheer rage of trying to beat those final levels.
