Review

This is one of the most polished Google Doodles ever made. The first thing that surprised me was the games style: pixel graphics like an indie game, with the design of a typical anime. The background music and sound effects also fit the style really well, and they create an atmosphere close to a big international sports festival. However, the game isnt really optimized for browsers on low-end devices (like Brave or Chrome). The most obvious problem is that moving around the whole map always causes serious lag, which makes the overall experience much worse. Anyway, this is one of the best Doodles I have ever seen in Google's history (and probably the last one i think).
