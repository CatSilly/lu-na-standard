Review

This is one of the games that has been connected to the history of my YouTube channel the most. Sometimes, I get mad at it, but because of the goals I set for myself in this game, I always come back to it like we were never separated. I started playing Geometry Dash around 2020. At that time, I didnt have enough money to buy the full version of the game, so I decided to play the demo version instead. Then, around July 28, 2024, I started uploading videos related to Geometry Dash, even though my skill was still pretty bad. I still remember that the game wasn't as popular as it is now. It gave my channel a pretty huge number of subscribers and quite a lot of viewers (although they werent loyal enough). However, developing games and building my own Studio took away a lot of my time, so I couldnt play GD as much as before. At the same time, I also got tired of playing the game I loved, which sounds like a very stupid problem when you think about it. So I decided to leave it behind and focus on other things. Even after leaving the game, I still kept up with the community and its news quite often. And I guess that says something: I still havent escaped from this game. In general, Geometry Dash is a game that is really worth trying for people who love rhythm-based games... and people who enjoy punching their desks more often.
