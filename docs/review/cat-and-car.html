Review

First of all, the gameplay is actually quite simple and relatively easy to understand for newcomers like me. For the graphics, with the pixel art style that is common in many indie games, along with many cute details, I quite like it overall. For the music, I also like how the developer combines the sound effects and background music together. These combinations create an atmosphere that really feels like an indie game. However, the game also has quite a few bugs (maybe it's because of my device), such as blurry fonts, character sprites being squeezed close together, and some other issues. Overall, this is one of those games that is great for killing time and is quite well-polished in terms of graphics. It deserves to be known by more people in the future.
