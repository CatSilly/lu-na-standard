Review

This is an excellent game to kill time, thanks to its minimalist yet incredibly addictive gameplay. The core gameplay loop is highly accessible: you just need to drag the matching items to the creature based on the given conditions. Notably, the game is very well-optimized, allowing lower-end devices to enjoy a smooth experience.
