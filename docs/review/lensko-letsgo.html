Review

This is one of my all-time favorite songs from NCS. The opening beat is so perfectly crafted that it immediately makes me want to move. However, the biggest surprise lies near the middle, where the music transitions and transports me into another dimension. I don't know what instrument the producer used, but it creates a mesmerizing, echoing sound. On top of that, the track brings back a wave of nostalgia from 2017. It’s a truly indescribable feeling...
